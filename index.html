LifeBalance: Heat & Water

A daily water goal that follows today's heat where you live.

LifeBalance sets your goal from your body and from the day's forecast, and reminds you more often on hot afternoons. Made for India. Free, with no ads and no account.

What you get

  • Heat-aware goalThe app checks the forecast for your place and shows a heat level: Normal, Warm, Hot or Extreme. The goal rises 10 to 30 percent with the heat level, up to a limit of 5,000 ml.
  • Drinks you actually haveWater, ORS, buttermilk, coconut water, nimbu pani, tea and coffee. Every drink counts at its full volume.
  • Adaptive remindersOff until you turn them on. They run only between your wake and sleep times, skip you if you drank recently, and come more often on Hot and Extreme days.
  • Home screen widgetSee your progress and add 250 ml with one tap.
  • Streak and 7-day chartEach day keeps the goal it was measured against.
  • Health Connect, optionalOff until you turn it on. It can read your steps to adjust the goal and save the water you log.
  • English and HindiUse the app in the language you prefer.
  • Your place, your choiceSearch for a city, or tap "Use my location" for your approximate area.

How it works

  1. Tell it about youEnter your weight, height, age, gender and activity level once.
  2. Pick your placeSearch your city, or use your approximate location.
  3. Log a drinkOne tap per drink. The goal follows the day's heat.

The heat level is the app's own estimate from weather forecasts. It is not an official warning. For official warnings see your national or local weather authority. LifeBalance gives a goal and general tips, not medical advice.

Private by design

No account, no ads and no analytics. Your profile and water log stay on your phone. Only an approximate location (rounded to about 11 km) or the city you searched is sent to MET Norway to get the forecast. In India, your state name (no coordinates) is sent to data.gov.in for air quality. City search stays on your device. Nothing else leaves your device. You can delete everything at any time from Settings. Read the privacy policy or see how to delete your data.
