LifeBalance: Water & Wellness

Your whole day in balance, tuned to Indian heat.

Track water, movement, sleep, mind and habits, and see them come together as one daily Balance score. Hot day? Your water goal rises and the app points out cooler hours to move. Made for India. Free, with no ads and no account.

What you get

  • One daily Balance scoreWater, Move, Sleep, Mind and Habits feed a single score on your Today screen, with insights and achievements.
  • Heat-smartThe app checks the forecast for your place. The water goal rises 10 to 30 percent with the heat level, and it shows the best time to move today.
  • Water and drinks you actually haveWater, ORS, buttermilk, coconut water, nimbu pani, tea and coffee. Every drink counts at its full volume.
  • Movement, sleep and mindSteps and workouts, nightly sleep, a quick mood check-in and guided breathing.
  • Your own habitsDaily habits with streaks, fruit and veg, meals and weight.
  • Family challengesAdd family members on one phone and race each other on water, movement, sleep, check-ins, habits or Balance. Share a card when you win. Names stay hidden unless you show them.
  • Wear OS, widget and remindersA watch tile with your water and Balance score, a home screen widget, and adaptive reminders that are off until you turn them on.
  • Optional Health Connect and Drive backupBoth are off until you turn them on. Health Connect can read steps, exercise and sleep. Drive backup saves to a hidden folder in your own Google Drive.
  • English and HindiUse the app in the language you prefer.
  • Free, with an optional tip jarNo ads, no account, no subscription. If it helps you, you can leave a tip in Settings. It unlocks nothing.

How it works

  1. Tell it about youEnter your weight, height, age, gender and activity level once.
  2. Pick your placeSearch your city, or use your approximate location.
  3. Log your dayOne tap for a drink, a quick workout, your sleep or your mood. The score updates as you go.

The heat level is the app's own estimate from weather forecasts. It is not an official warning. For official warnings see your national or local weather authority. LifeBalance gives goals and general tips, not medical advice.

Private by design

No account, no ads and no analytics. Your data stays on your phone. Only an approximate location (rounded to about 11 km) or the city you searched is sent to MET Norway to get the forecast. In India, your state name (no coordinates) is sent to data.gov.in for air quality. City search stays on your device. Nothing else leaves your device. You can delete everything at any time from Settings. Read the privacy policy or see how to delete your data.
