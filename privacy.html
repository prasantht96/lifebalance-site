Privacy Policy for LifeBalance

Last Updated: October 15, 2026

Introduction

LifeBalance ("we", "our", or "the App") is a hydration app that sets a daily water goal that follows the weather where you are. This Privacy Policy explains what the App stores, what leaves your device, and the choices you have. The App has no account, no ads and no analytics.

Information Collection and Use

Data stored on your device

LifeBalance stores the following on your device only:

  • Profile: weight, height, age, gender and activity level, used to calculate your water goal.
  • Water log: the amount, drink type and time of each drink you log, and the goal for each day.
  • Settings: your preferences, such as reminders, quick-add amounts, units and language.
  • Chosen place: the city you picked, or the approximate area found with "Use my location", with its coordinates rounded to about 11 km.
  • Cached forecast: the most recent weather forecast, so the App can work offline.

We do not receive any of this. It is never sent to us.

What leaves your device

To work out today's heat level, the App sends a request to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com), a third-party weather service. The request contains:

  • Approximate coordinates, rounded to about 11 km (0.1 degrees). They come either from the city you search for or, only if you tap "Use my location" or turn on "Update with my location", from your device's approximate (coarse) location. Open-Meteo uses them to return the weather forecast and air quality for that area.
  • The city search text you type, sent to Open-Meteo's geocoding service to find matching places.

Nothing else is sent. The App sends no name, no email, no account identifier, no advertising ID and no precise location. Like any internet request, Open-Meteo's servers can see your device's IP address. Open-Meteo is an independent service with its own terms and privacy policy: Open-Meteo Terms and Privacy. Weather data by Open-Meteo.com, licensed under CC BY 4.0.

The place name shown for your location is looked up on your device from a built-in list of cities (place names by GeoNames.org, CC BY 4.0); nothing is sent for it.

We do not sell your data or share it for advertising. Our servers are not involved; the App talks to Open-Meteo directly from your device.

Location permission

  • The App asks only for approximate (coarse) location. It never asks for precise location.
  • It asks only when you tap "Use my location" or turn on "Update with my location". It reads your location only while the App is open: once when you tap the button, and each time you open the App if that setting is on.
  • It never uses location in the background.
  • The setting "Update with my location" is off by default. If you turn it on, the App re-checks your approximate location when you open it.
  • You can decline the permission and search for a city instead.

Notifications

Reminders are optional and off by default. If you turn them on, they are scheduled on your device and do not need a server. On Android 13 and later the App asks for notification permission when you turn reminders on.

Health Connect (optional)

Health Connect is optional, off by default, and available on Android 9 and later. If you turn it on and grant permission:

  • the App reads your step count to adjust today's water goal, and
  • the App writes the water you log into Health Connect as hydration records.

This data stays on your device inside Health Connect. We never receive it, and the App does not send it to Open-Meteo or anyone else. You can change or remove the permissions at any time in Health Connect. Records the App has written stay in Health Connect until you delete them there.

The use of information received from Health Connect will adhere to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

No cloud backup

The App opts out of Android backup, so your data is not copied to Google Drive or other cloud backup. If you move to a new phone, your LifeBalance data does not move with you.

Data Deletion

LifeBalance has no user accounts. Your profile, water log and settings exist only on your device, and we hold none of it.

There is no data deletion request process, because we hold no data to delete. You do not need to contact us or submit a request. You can delete all of it yourself at any time, and the deletion is immediate and permanent.

How to delete all of your data

Any of the following permanently erases your LifeBalance data from your device:

  • Delete all data in the App. Open Settings → Delete all data. This erases your profile, water log, settings, chosen place and cached forecast immediately, cancels reminders, and returns the App to the first-time setup.
  • Uninstall the app. Removing LifeBalance deletes its local storage.
  • Clear the app's data without uninstalling. On Android, open Settings → Apps → LifeBalance → Storage → Clear data (the exact wording varies by device).

Water records the App wrote to Health Connect stay in Health Connect. You can delete them in the Health Connect app.

Once deleted, this data cannot be recovered by you or by us. We hold no backups, copies or archives of it.

If you have any question about deleting your data, email us at prasantht246@gmail.com and we will respond. See also the data deletion page.

Third-Party Services

The only third-party service the App contacts is Open-Meteo, for the weather forecast, air quality and city search described above. The App has no analytics, advertising or tracking services. If you use Health Connect, that data is handled by Android's Health Connect under its own terms.

Not Medical Advice

LifeBalance gives a water goal and general tips. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose or treat any condition. If you have a heart or kidney condition, or any other medical condition, ask your doctor how much to drink. The heat levels shown in the App are the App's own estimate calculated from weather forecasts. They are not official government warnings. For official heat warnings, check your national or local weather authority.

Children's Privacy

LifeBalance does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The App collects no personal information from anyone; it is intended for general audiences to track their daily water intake.

Data Security

Your data is stored on your device, so its security depends on your device's security settings. We recommend using a device passcode or biometric lock and keeping your operating system updated. Requests to Open-Meteo are sent over HTTPS.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with a new "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.

Your Rights

  • Access: view your data anytime within the App.
  • Modify: update your profile and settings at any time.
  • Delete: erase everything at any time with Settings → Delete all data, by uninstalling, or by clearing the App's data. See the Data Deletion section above.
