Delete your data

Last updated: October 15, 2026

LifeBalance (by Prasanth T) has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of ours. Your profile, water log and settings exist only on your phone, and we hold none of it. There is no data deletion request process, because we hold no data to delete. You do not need to contact us or submit a request.

How to delete all of your data

Any of the following permanently erases your LifeBalance data from your device, immediately:

  • Delete all data in the app: open Settings → Delete all data. This erases your profile, water log, settings, chosen place and cached forecast, cancels reminders, and returns the app to the first-time setup.
  • Uninstall the app: removing LifeBalance deletes its local storage.
  • Clear the app's data without uninstalling: on Android, open Settings → Apps → LifeBalance → Storage → Clear data (the exact wording varies by device).

Health Connect records

If you turned on the optional Health Connect link, water records the app wrote to Health Connect stay in Health Connect. Delete them in the Health Connect app. You can also remove LifeBalance's permissions there at any time.

What about Open-Meteo?

The app sends approximate coordinates (rounded to about 11 km) or your city search text to Open-Meteo to get the forecast. We do not receive or store these. See the privacy policy for details.

Once deleted, this data cannot be recovered by you or by us. We hold no backups, copies or archives of it.
