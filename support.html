Frequently asked questions

How is my daily goal calculated?

The goal starts from your weight, height, age, gender and activity level. On top of that, the heat level for your place today raises it by 10 to 30 percent. The goal is capped at 5,000 ml. If you turn on Health Connect, a high step count on an active day can raise it a little more. You can also set your own goal by hand in Settings.

What do the heat levels mean?

Each day the app looks at the forecast for your place and shows one of four levels: Normal, Warm, Hot or Extreme. The levels are our own estimate calculated from weather forecasts. They are not official warnings. For official heat warnings, check your national or local weather authority, for example mausam.imd.gov.in in India.

Why does the app want my location?

It does not have to. You can search for your city instead. If you tap "Use my location", the app asks for approximate (coarse) location only, reads it once while the app is open, and rounds it to about 11 km before it is used. If you turn on "Update with my location", it re-checks each time you open the app. It never uses location in the background, and it never asks for precise location.

Why are my reminders not showing?

Reminders are off until you turn them on in Settings. If they are on but do not appear, check these:

  • Notification permission: on Android 13 and later, allow notifications for LifeBalance in Settings → Apps → LifeBalance → Notifications.
  • Battery optimisation: some phones stop background work to save battery. Open Settings → Apps → LifeBalance → Battery and choose Unrestricted (or Not optimised). Phone makers name this differently.
  • Wake and sleep times: reminders only come between them, and are skipped if you logged a drink recently.
  • Do Not Disturb: check that it is not silencing the app.
How do I add the widget?

Long-press an empty area of your home screen, choose Widgets, find LifeBalance and drag it to the screen. The widget shows your progress and has a button that adds 250 ml in one tap. Steps vary a little by phone maker.

How does Health Connect work?

It is optional and off by default. If you turn it on and grant permission, the app reads your step count to adjust today's goal and writes the water you log into Health Connect. This data stays on your device. You can change the permissions at any time in Health Connect, and records the app wrote stay there until you delete them in Health Connect. See the privacy policy.

How do I move to a new phone?

Your data is not backed up. LifeBalance opts out of Android backup on purpose, so nothing is copied to Google Drive or any server. On a new phone you start again with a fresh profile. Your log does not move with you.

Does it cost anything, or show ads?

No. LifeBalance is free, with no ads, no account and no subscription.
